representative 3-day periods; the price check maps the weighted rep-period price duration curve (plus monthly means and
daily spreads) to the real-world PDC. Full-year fixed-fleet dispatch is only the one-off check of period selection.
Every feature is one block (switch, labelled rows, audit hooks, price-setter class). Every gate from 4 on reports
size/runtime in `reports/performance-ledger.md` against the budget.
Other RTOs/grids added later, one at a time. First use case: **storage value**.
Focus throughout: **benchmarking / backcast accuracy**. From Gate 5 on, every feature has
an ablation switch, a complexity-ledger row, and must hold or improve its benchmark.
